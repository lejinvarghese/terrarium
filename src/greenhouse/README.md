# Greenhouse 🌱

The experimental space for growing new bots and testing AI capabilities.

## Purpose

The greenhouse is where new bot personalities are developed, tested, and refined before being integrated into the terrarium ecosystem. Think of it as an incubator for AI intelligence—a place for experimentation, simulation, and growth.

## Future Vision

This space may include:
- **Bot Development** - Prototyping new bot personalities and capabilities
- **Testing Environments** - Sandboxed spaces to test bot behaviors
- **Simulation Engines** - Tools to model bot interactions and responses
- **Training Data** - Datasets and prompts for bot refinement
- **Experiments** - Research into new AI patterns and architectures

## Current Status

🚧 **Under Construction** - This space is currently empty and ready for your experiments.

## Getting Started

When you're ready to grow a new bot:
1. Create a prototype personality file
2. Test interactions in isolation
3. Refine capabilities based on feedback
4. Graduate successful bots to `.claude/agents/` for production use

The greenhouse is yours to explore—experiment freely! 🌿
